# September 28th: Designed the weapon
Today, I worked alot on the actual weapon. I had to consider the size of the tooth and the size of the counterweight. I focused alot on fixing the thickness and making sure the center of mass was close to the origon, I had to teach myself alot of this stuff so it took alot longer than usual but I learned how to add the end of the day. I also imported the motor in to demstrate what it would look like, getting the file from a friend.
